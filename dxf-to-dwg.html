How to convert DXF to DWG

Our converters create DXF files, the universal CAD exchange format. Turning a DXF into a DWG takes a few seconds.

In AutoCAD

  1. Open AutoCAD and open the DXF file (File → Open, set file type to DXF).
  2. Type SAVEAS and press Enter.
  3. Choose AutoCAD 2018 Drawing (*.dwg) or the version your team uses.
  4. Click Save. You now have a DWG.

Free, without AutoCAD

  • ODA File Converter (free from the Open Design Alliance) batch-converts DXF to DWG on Windows, Mac and Linux.
  • DraftSight and BricsCAD (trial) open DXF and save DWG.
  • LibreCAD (free) opens and edits DXF directly — often you don't need DWG at all.

Tips after converting

  • Type ZOOM → Extents if you can't see the drawing.
  • Use SCALE → Reference to fix the scale from one known dimension.
  • Use OVERKILL to remove duplicate lines, and JOIN to merge polylines.
  • Lines are on layers LINES, FILLS and TEXT so you can switch groups on and off.
