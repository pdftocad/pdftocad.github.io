Frequently asked questions

What images work best?

Clean black-and-white line drawings, scans at 300 dpi, and logos. Photos of paper sketches work too — take them straight on, in good light.

Why do lines come out as double lines?

The tracer draws the outline of each black shape, so a thick line becomes a thin closed shape. That is normal for image tracing and keeps the exact look of the original.

How do I set the right size?

Enter the real width of the drawing in millimetres. If you know one real dimension, you can also use SCALE with the Reference option in AutoCAD after opening.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Tracing runs entirely in your browser, so your images stay on your device.

Can it convert a photo of a building?

It converts shapes by contrast, so photos become outlines of dark areas. It works best on drawings, plans, sketches, signatures and logos.
