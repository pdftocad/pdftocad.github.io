How to convert

  1. Drop your PDF onto the box or click to choose it.
  2. Pick the page, and enter the drawing scale if you want real-world units (e.g. 100 for 1:100).
  3. Click Convert to DXF and check the preview.
  4. Download the DXF and open it in AutoCAD, BricsCAD, LibreCAD or any CAD program.

Frequently asked questions

Does it make a DWG file?

It makes a DXF file, the open CAD exchange format. AutoCAD, BricsCAD, DraftSight, LibreCAD, Revit and SketchUp all open DXF directly. In AutoCAD, open the DXF and use Save As → DWG — it takes five seconds. See our DXF to DWG guide.

Is my drawing uploaded?

No. The conversion runs inside your browser on your own computer. Your PDF never leaves your device, so confidential project drawings stay private.

Why is my scanned PDF empty?

A scanned PDF is just a picture of a drawing — it has no lines to extract. Use the Image to CAD converter, which traces the picture into lines.

Will the drawing be to scale?

Yes. With scale 1 the DXF is the paper size in millimetres. If your PDF is a 1:100 drawing, enter 100 and every dimension comes out in real-world millimetres.

What gets converted?

Lines, polylines, arcs and curves (as polylines), rectangles, filled shapes and text, each on its own layer (LINES, FILLS, TEXT) with colours kept close to the original.

Does it work with PDFs from Revit or ArchiCAD?

Yes — any vector PDF works, whether it was printed from AutoCAD, Revit, ArchiCAD, MicroStation, SolidWorks or an office program.
