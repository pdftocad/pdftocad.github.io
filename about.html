About PDFtoCAD

PDFtoCAD is a free converter made by a BIM engineer who got tired of redrawing PDFs. It turns vector PDF drawings and images into editable CAD (DXF) files.

Everything runs inside your web browser — your drawings are never uploaded to any server, which makes it safe for confidential project files.

The site is free and supported by tips. If it saves you time, you can buy us a coffee on Ko-fi.
